Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2025

Ardu ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use the Ardu mobile application ("the App").

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Information: If you sign in using Google or Apple, we receive a unique user identifier and basic profile information (such as name or email, depending on the provider).
  • User Content: Quotes you create, favorite, or request (including AI-generated quotes).
  • Device Information: Basic device data such as operating system, app version, and anonymous identifiers used for analytics and advertising.
  • Usage Data: Interaction with the App, such as viewed quotes, feature usage, and crash reports.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide and improve the App’s features
  • Deliver motivational quotes and notifications
  • Generate AI-powered quotes when requested
  • Maintain account functionality and preferences
  • Display advertisements
  • Monitor performance and fix bugs

Push Notifications

Ardu may send push notifications containing motivational quotes. Notifications are only sent after you grant permission and can be disabled at any time in your device settings.

Advertising

The App may display ads provided by third-party advertising partners (such as Google AdMob). These partners may use device identifiers to show relevant ads.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data may be shared only with trusted third-party services required to operate the App, including:

  • Authentication providers (Google, Apple)
  • Cloud storage and backend services
  • Analytics and crash reporting tools
  • Advertising networks

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data using industry-standard security practices. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained only as long as necessary to provide the App’s functionality. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

Children’s Privacy

Ardu is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, update, or delete your personal data. You may also withdraw consent for notifications or advertising tracking through your device settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
